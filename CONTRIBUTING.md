# Contributing
- Synthetic data only. Never submit real product/site/batch/patient/employer data in issues, PRs, or gold items.
- New gold items must validate against schema/item.schema.json and belong to one of the six axes.
- To add a leaderboard row: include system, model+date, condition, τ, and the four headline numbers, with a link to your preds file.
- No claim of regulator acceptance or production validation from a score.
